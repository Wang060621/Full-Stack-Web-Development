# Brand direction: GrooveGavel

## Theme

GrooveGavel is a specialist marketplace for storied objects: timepieces, cameras, instruments, travel pieces and decorative antiques. The visual direction combines the intimacy of a private collector’s cabinet with the clarity expected from a trustworthy auction platform.

## Identity

- Brand name: **GrooveGavel**
- Tagline: **Old things. New stories.**
- Primary colour: Oxblood wine `#681F2C`
- Secondary colour: Aged brass `#B88B45`
- Background: Warm parchment `#EFE2CA`
- Surface: Light parchment `#FBF5E8`
- Text: Cabinet ink `#1D1712`
- Success: Bottle green `#345C46`
- Error: Brick crimson `#982F3B`

## Typography

- Headings: an editorial old-style serif, with `Georgia` as the dependable fallback
- Body copy: `Georgia`, with `Times New Roman` as fallback
- Navigation, forms and labels: a neutral system sans-serif, with `Arial` as fallback
- Prices and countdowns: tabular numerals for rapid comparison

## Image style

Use real, warm editorial photographs of individual antique objects on restrained backgrounds. Product images should be square, consistently cropped, and free of decorative text. Keep image credits and reuse terms with the assets. Avoid mixed filters, busy collages, generated product stand-ins, and low-resolution marketplace screenshots.

## UI principles

- Make current bid, closing time, and condition visible without opening an item.
- Reserve oxblood for brand recognition and primary actions; use brass sparingly for rules, stamps and highlights.
- Never communicate auction state by colour alone; pair colour with an icon and text.
- Keep focus rings, labels, error summaries, and keyboard navigation visible.
