# Brand direction: GrooveGavel

## Theme

GrooveGavel is a specialist marketplace for collectible vinyl records. The visual direction combines the warmth of record shops with the clarity expected from a trustworthy auction platform.

## Identity

- Brand name: **GrooveGavel**
- Tagline: **Rare grooves. Fair bids.**
- Primary colour: Deep violet `#6D28D9`
- Secondary colour: Warm amber `#F59E0B`
- Background: Soft cream `#FFFBEB`
- Text: Ink `#18181B`
- Success: Emerald `#047857`
- Error: Crimson `#BE123C`

## Typography

- Headings: **Space Grotesk**, with `Arial` as fallback
- Body and forms: **Inter**, with `Arial` as fallback
- Prices and countdowns: tabular numerals for rapid comparison

## Image style

Use warm, high-contrast editorial photographs of record sleeves and turntables on simple backgrounds. Product images should be square, consistently cropped, and free of decorative text. Avoid mixed filters, busy collages, and low-resolution marketplace screenshots.

## UI principles

- Make current bid, closing time, and condition visible without opening an item.
- Reserve amber for calls to action and violet for navigation/brand recognition.
- Never communicate auction state by colour alone; pair colour with an icon and text.
- Keep focus rings, labels, error summaries, and keyboard navigation visible.
